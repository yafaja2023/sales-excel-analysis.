
# Superstore Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data using Microsoft Excel
to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance,
and customer behavior.

The project includes data analysis using PivotTables, PivotCharts,
KPI cards, and interactive slicers to create an interactive dashboard.

## 2. Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify sales trends over time.
- Compare sales and profit across categories and regions.
- Identify the best- and worst-performing products.
- Identify the most valuable customers.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard to support decision-making.

## 3. Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Slicers
- Data visualization

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The following KPIs were calculated:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

## 5. Analysis Performed

### Sales Analysis
- Sales by Year
- Sales by Month
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Segment

### Profit Analysis
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Profit by Region

### Product Analysis
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit

### Customer Analysis
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Top 10 Customers by Profit

## 6. Dashboard Features
- KPI cards displaying key business metrics.
- Interactive slicers for filtering the analysis.
- Charts showing sales and profit trends.
- Product and customer performance comparisons.
- Dynamic visualizations that respond to user selections.


## 7. Key Findings

- **Sales Trends:** Sales generally increased over the years, with noticeable
  monthly fluctuations and seasonal patterns.

- **Most Profitable Category:** Technology and Office Supplies are important
  contributors to profitability. The category with the highest total profit
  was identified through the profit analysis.

- **Regional Performance:** Sales and profitability varied across regions,
  highlighting differences in regional business performance.

- **Product Performance:** Some products generated low or negative profits,
  indicating potential issues with discounting, pricing, or costs.

- **Customer Analysis:** A small group of customers contributed significantly
  to total sales and profit, making them valuable customers for the business.

- **Sales and Profit Relationship:** High sales did not always result in high
  profits, showing the importance of analyzing profitability alongside revenue.


## 8. Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:

- Focus on products and categories that generate high profits.
- Investigate products with low or negative profitability.
- Explore sales trends to identify seasonal opportunities.
- Develop strategies to improve performance in weaker regions.
- Strengthen relationships with high-value customers.
